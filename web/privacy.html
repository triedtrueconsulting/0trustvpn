0trustvpn / privacy

Privacy policy.

Effective 2026-05 · Version 1.0

This is the legally binding version of the claims in our no-logs audit pack. Where this policy and our marketing ever appear to disagree, this policy and the audit pack govern. We have written it to be specific and falsifiable rather than reassuring and vague.

1. Who we are

0trust ("we", "us") operates a privacy-focused mesh VPN service at 0trustvpn.com. We run three server nodes, in Switzerland, Iceland, and Romania — all outside the Five, Nine, and Fourteen Eyes intelligence-sharing alliances. Contact: 0trustvpn@proton.me (PGP key at /.well-known/security.txt).

2. What we collect

The complete list of what we store:

  • A SHA-256 hash of your account token (never the token itself)
  • Your devices' WireGuard public keys and assigned mesh IPs (required to route packets to you)
  • Your chosen region(s) and routing mode
  • Your subscription expiry date and the invoice ID + USD amount for ~30 days (refund window)
  • 2 days of web-server access logs, with IP addresses truncated to the /24 (IPv4) or /48 (IPv6) before they are written
  • Up to 7 days of application error logs on the control plane (tokens are hashed before logging; device keys never appear)

That is the entire list. If a category of data is not named above, we do not store it.

3. What we never collect

  • Your real IP address (we never write it un-truncated)
  • The websites or services you visit through the VPN
  • The amount of data you transfer (bandwidth counters are disabled)
  • The times you connect or disconnect, or session durations
  • Your name, email, or phone number — we never ask for them
  • Your cryptocurrency wallet address (our payment processor handles payment; the control plane only learns "invoice paid: yes/no")
  • Any device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics

5. We use no third-party trackers

Our website serves no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party scripts beyond self-hosted code. The only external origins our pages contact are Google Fonts (for typefaces); we are migrating those to self-hosting. We do not use cookies for tracking. The single piece of client-side state is your account token, which never leaves your device except as a hash sent to authenticate API requests.

6. Legal requests & the warrant canary

Our structural defense against legal compulsion is that we don't hold the data most requests seek — there is nothing to hand over. Where a valid legal order in one of our jurisdictions compels us to act, we will comply with the law while disclosing as little as the law requires, and we will never voluntarily provide data we are not legally compelled to provide.

We publish a warrant canary, PGP-signed and re-signed weekly. If it stops being updated, treat its absence as a signal. We cannot lawfully promise the canary will always be accurate; that is precisely why it is structured as a canary rather than a promise.

7. Server seizure

Our mesh nodes are RAM-only ephemeral instances: their disk is a RAM-backed filesystem, swap is disabled, and WireGuard keys regenerate on every reboot. A physically seized node contains its base operating system image and nothing operationally relevant about customers. The control plane stores account tokens only as SHA-256 hashes.

8. Data retention & deletion

Web access logs: 2 days, then deleted automatically. Application error logs: 7 days. Account records: until your subscription expires, plus the refund window, after which expired records are purged by an automated sweeper. You can hasten deletion by simply not renewing — there is no account to "close" because there is no identity attached to it. Because we cannot identify which token is yours, we cannot perform a per-user data export or deletion on request; the privacy architecture that protects you also means we have nothing user-specific to export.

9. Children

The service is not directed at anyone under 18. We collect no information that would let us identify a user's age, and we do not knowingly provide service to minors.

10. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced in the warrant canary and reflected in the version number above. Because we have no way to email you (we don't have your email), the canary and this page are the canonical notice channel. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.

11. Independent verification

Don't take our word for any of this. The audit pack documents every claim with the verification commands that confirm it, and ships a script (scripts/audit-verify.sh) that an independent auditor — or you, if you self-host — can run against a live server to check that what we say matches what the server does.

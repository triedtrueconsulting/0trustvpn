# Everything public is crawlable. Two things are not, and both for the same
# reason rather than any wish to be hidden.

User-agent: *
Allow: /

# The visitor proxy renders OTHER people's pages under our origin. Letting a
# crawler index that would put third-party content on our domain, invite
# duplicate-content penalties for pages we do not own, and make us look like
# a content farm. The service sends X-Robots-Tag: noindex on every response
# as well; this is the belt to that pair of braces.
Disallow: /surf

# The operations dashboard. It can ban customers and move money.
Disallow: /admin

# AI answer engines are welcome, deliberately. A VPN is bought on trust, and
# the questions people ask an assistant about one — what is logged, who runs
# the servers, which jurisdiction, can it be audited — are exactly the
# questions this site answers in public, in specific and checkable terms. We
# would rather those answers came from our own pages than from a review site
# that guessed. The same two exclusions apply, for the same reasons.
User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /

§ 01 · Operating Principles

We can't hand over what we
never collected.

→ 01

No identity.

Accounts are random sixteen-character tokens. No email, no phone, no name. The token is the credential. Lose it, you lose access. We can't reset it because we don't know who you are.

→ 02

No logs.

Connection metadata is discarded at session close. Traffic content is never inspected. Diskless servers run from RAM and reset on reboot. Audited yearly by independent firms.

→ 03

No trust.

Zero-trust mesh routing means every hop authenticates every packet. No entry node sees both you and your destination. Peer rotation is automatic and key material is ephemeral.

→ 04

Crypto first.

Bitcoin. No name, no email, no account with anyone — but Bitcoin is pseudonymous, not anonymous: every payment is on a public chain forever, and if the coins came from an exchange that knows you, that link exists whatever we do. A fresh address per invoice is the most we can do from our side. Cards are available where the operator has enabled them, and they are not private at all: the processor learns who you are and that you bought a VPN. What that means →

→ 05

Open client.

The client is open-source and reproducibly built. It uses the WireGuard kernel module or the userspace implementation. Verify everything; trust nothing. Including us.

→ 06

Plain price.

Five dollars per month, paid in any supported coin at current market rate. No upsells, no tiers, no "premium." One price for everyone, forever, until it isn't.

§ 03 · Where our servers live

Three countries.
Zero Eyes.

We deliberately run only three nodes, in three countries, all outside the Five Eyes, Nine Eyes, and Fourteen Eyes intelligence- sharing alliances. Fewer locations means more scrutiny on each one. Each box is a RAM-only ephemeral instance — disk is tmpfs, swap is off, keys regenerate on every reboot.

🇨🇭
Switzerland
zur · Zurich
  • Outside all Five/Nine/Fourteen Eyes alliances
  • Constitutional right to telecommunications privacy (Article 13)
  • Strong data-protection law (revFADP, 2023)
  • Federal Supreme Court has repeatedly ruled against blanket retention
🇮🇸
Iceland
rey · Reykjavik
  • Outside all Eyes alliances; not in EU
  • IMMI (Icelandic Modern Media Initiative) baseline
  • No mandatory data retention regime for ISPs or hosts
  • Strong source-protection law (Act 38/2011)
🇷🇴
Romania
buc · Bucharest
  • Outside all Eyes alliances
  • Constitutional Court struck down EU data retention directive (2014)
  • No general comms-data retention law currently in force
  • Long-standing privacy-VPN industry presence; well-understood regime
What we explicitly don't do. No exit nodes in any Eyes country. No servers in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Germany, France, Netherlands, Denmark, Belgium, Italy, Spain, Sweden, or Norway. Not for latency, not for redundancy, not for any reason.

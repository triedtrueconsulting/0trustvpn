If you still need help, please re-send with this in mind:

  1. We literally cannot identify you from your email address. The only
     identifier we have is your 16-character account token. If your
     question is account-specific, mention your token in the body.

  2. We can read PGP-encrypted mail. Our key is at
     https://0trustvpn.com/.well-known/openpgpkey/hu/... and on
     keys.openpgp.org. If your issue involves anything sensitive
     (the full token, payment IDs, your tunnel logs), please encrypt.

  3. Don't send screenshots that include your IP address, real name,
     or other identifying info. We can almost always help without it.
     If we genuinely need it, we'll ask explicitly.

  4. If this is a security vulnerability, please re-send to
     0trustvpn@proton.me (encrypted to the same key). We commit to
     acknowledging vuln reports within 24 hours and never publishing
     anything that could endanger researchers.
